Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Welcome to Shanvi Digital Seva Kendra ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our physical shop or our website at shanvidigital.in.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when requesting our digital, printing, or e-governance services. This may include:

  • Name, Phone Number, and Email Address.
  • Documents provided for digital applications (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID).
  • Files, images, or documents sent via WhatsApp or Email for custom printing (like PVC cards, photo frames, or notebooks).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the purpose of fulfilling the specific services you have requested, which includes:

  • Processing government portal applications (PAN, Voter ID, RTPS certificates).
  • Completing digital banking transactions (AEPS) and ticket bookings.
  • Fulfilling custom printing and manufacturing orders.

3. Data Protection and Retention (Important)

We take your privacy very seriously. We process customer requests in person and do not store sensitive personal or financial information on our local systems after the service is completed.

Any documents, images, or files sent to us by customers via WhatsApp or Email for printing purposes are permanently deleted immediately after the print job is finished.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with official, secure government or banking portals (such as CSC, UTI, NSDL) strictly as required to complete your requested online applications.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
